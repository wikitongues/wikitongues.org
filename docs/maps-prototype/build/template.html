02 · Disputed borders

Neutral, everywhere

Disputed areas belong to no one on these maps. Each gets a dark dashed edge on a parchment casing and a hatch. The hatch takes the language's colour when a claimant is one of its territories, so a Ukrainian page shows Crimea as contested, not as lost. Natural Earth's de facto and US views were compared and dropped.
